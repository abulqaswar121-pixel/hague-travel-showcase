HAGUE TRAVEL ACADEMY
Travel Consultant Masterclass — Cohort Syllabus (2027)
=====================================================

Delivery: 8 live modules, recordings for 12 months, practical labs and a final case study.
Cohorts: February, May, August and November.
Taught hours: 45. Class size: maximum 30. Pass mark: 60%.

MODULE 1 — Introduction to travel & tourism
- The travel value chain: airlines, hotels, DMCs, OTAs, TMCs and agencies
- Roles, career paths and the daily rhythm of a consultant
- Professional ethics and the limits of what we can promise
Outcome: explain how money, risk and responsibility move through a booking.

MODULE 2 — Flight booking fundamentals
- Airport and airline codes, routes, connections and minimum connect times
- Fare families, baggage, taxes, surcharges and the difference between a quote and a ticket
- Reading a PNR and understanding the ticketing time limit
Outcome: build a compliant itinerary and explain the fare conditions to a client.

MODULE 3 — Hotel reservations & package planning
- Room types, rate plans, board basis and cancellation windows
- Building a package from separate supplier components
- Transfers, excursions and pre/post-stay nights
Outcome: price and document a package without hiding supplier terms.

MODULE 4 — Customer service & itinerary management
- Discovery questions, expectation setting and written confirmations
- Changes, disruption and the rebooking conversation
- Complaint handling that keeps the client and the supplier whole
Outcome: manage a live file from first enquiry to post-trip follow-up.

MODULE 5 — Visa documentation & ethics
- The difference between advice, document review and a decision
- Financial evidence, sponsorship letters and credibility interviews
- Refusal analysis and honest reapplication planning
Outcome: audit a visa file against a country checklist and explain the gaps.

MODULE 6 — Tour operations & suppliers
- Supplier contracts, deposits, release dates and cancellation penalties
- Group movement manifests, rooming lists and seat deposits
- On-tour risk, duty of care and emergency escalation
Outcome: operate a group departure without losing a rooming list or a passenger.

MODULE 7 — Travel business setup & sales
- Agency registration, IATA/ARC considerations and consortium options
- Pricing for margin, service fees and commissions
- Sales conversations, proposals and repeat business
Outcome: produce a one-page business plan for a travel desk.

MODULE 8 — Assessment & career preparation
- Final case study across flights, hotels, visas and a disruption
- CV, portfolio and interview preparation for travel roles
- Partner introductions and next-step plan
Outcome: a graded final file and a career action plan.
