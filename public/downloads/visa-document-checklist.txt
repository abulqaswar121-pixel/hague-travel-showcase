Visa Documentation — Consultant Checklist
=========================================

This checklist supports document review. It is not an approval, a legal opinion, or a promise
that a visa will be issued. The embassy or immigration office makes that decision.

IDENTITY
[ ] Passport bio page, clear and uncropped
[ ] Passport valid for the required period beyond the intended stay
[ ] Name spelling matches every form and supporting document
[ ] Previous passports or refusal letters, where requested
[ ] Birth certificate or marriage certificate, where the relationship is relevant

ADMISSIONS / PURPOSE
[ ] Offer letter or CAS / I-20 / acceptance letter
[ ] Course details, start date and tuition deposit receipt
[ ] Sponsor letter, where someone else is paying
[ ] Employment letter or business registration, where relevant

FINANCIAL EVIDENCE
[ ] Bank statements covering the required period (for example, the 28-day rule)
[ ] Statement is dated, stamped and shows the account holder's name
[ ] Source of funds is explainable and consistent with the sponsor's income
[ ] Loan or scholarship documentation, where applicable
[ ] No unexplained large deposits shortly before the application

TRAVEL & ACCOMMODATION
[ ] Flight itinerary or hold — not presented as a confirmed ticket
[ ] Accommodation confirmation or halls contract
[ ] Travel insurance, where required
[ ] Return or onward travel plan, where required

HEALTH & COMPLIANCE
[ ] TB test certificate, where the country requires it
[ ] Vaccination records, where required
[ ] Police clearance or medical report, where required

BEFORE SUBMISSION
[ ] Every answer is consistent across forms
[ ] Dates and amounts add up
[ ] The client has read and signed the declaration
[ ] A copy of the complete file is saved to the secure case vault
[ ] The client understands that the decision rests with the authority

RED FLAGS TO ESCALATE
- A document that looks altered or inconsistent
- A sponsor whose income cannot support the stated funds
- A refusal history that has not been disclosed
- Any request to "guarantee" an approval
