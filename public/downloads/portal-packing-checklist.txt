HAGUE TRAVEL — PRE-DEPARTURE CHECKLIST
=====================================

TRAVEL DOCUMENTS
[ ] Passport and visa (printed and saved offline)
[ ] Flight itinerary and ticket numbers
[ ] Accommodation confirmation and host contact
[ ] Travel insurance certificate and emergency number
[ ] Student or work permit approval letter
[ ] Copies stored separately from the originals

MONEY & CONNECTIVITY
[ ] Local currency or a card that works abroad
[ ] Bank notification for travel dates
[ ] Roaming or eSIM arranged
[ ] Emergency cash and a backup card
[ ] University or employer emergency contact

HEALTH & BAGGAGE
[ ] Prescriptions and a copy of the prescription
[ ] Adapter, chargers and a power bank in cabin baggage
[ ] Airline baggage rules checked and bags weighed
[ ] Warm clothing in the cabin for long-haul flights
[ ] Vaccination or TB certificates, where applicable

ARRIVAL WEEK
[ ] Airport pickup confirmed or public transport plan
[ ] Accommodation keys or check-in instructions
[ ] SIM, bank account and GP registration steps
[ ] Campus or workplace induction date
[ ] First-week grocery and transport budget

This is a general checklist, not immigration advice. Country-specific requirements are
confirmed in your pre-departure brief.
