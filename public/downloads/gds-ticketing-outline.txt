HAGUE TRAVEL ACADEMY
GDS Ticketing & Reservation — Practice Outline
==============================================

A hands-on add-on for consultants who need to build, price and service a PNR correctly.

LAB 1 — GDS orientation
- Sign-in, work areas and queues
- Encode/ decode airports, airlines, aircraft and cities
- Display schedules and availability

LAB 2 — Build and price a PNR
- Name, itinerary, contact and ticketing fields
- Fare quote, taxes, baggage and fare rules
- Ticketing time limits and queue placement

LAB 3 — Fare rules, taxes and voids
- Fare basis decode, conditions and penalties
- Tax breakdown and the difference between quoted and payable
- Void windows and the cost of missing one

LAB 4 — Changes, reissues and refunds
- Voluntary vs involuntary changes
- Reissue calculations and residual values
- Refund processing and debit-memo risk

PRACTICE CASE
Build a four-segment Lagos–Manchester family PNR. Two adults, one child. Check baggage,
seat requests, meal codes, fare conditions and the ticketing time limit. Document every
assumption in the client file.

Tools: training GDS environment or demo account supplied during the cohort.
Assessment: practical lab submission plus a short fare-rule quiz.
